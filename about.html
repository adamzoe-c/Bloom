Our Story

Grown From

Bloom & Co was born in a small atelier with one florist, a market stall and an obsession with beautiful flowers. What started as a weekend farmers market stand has grown into one of the city's most loved flower studios — yet our philosophy remains the same.

Every stem we sell is chosen by hand. Every arrangement is made fresh that morning. And every delivery is a small act of joy we take seriously.

What we stand for

Our Values

Sustainability First

We work exclusively with eco-certified growers who prioritise soil health, water conservation and zero single-use plastic in their operations.

Made With Love

Every bouquet is a creative work. Our florists pour genuine care into each arrangement — no assembly lines, no shortcuts.

Reliable Every Time

We deliver on our promises — literally. A 96% same-day delivery rate isn't something we're satisfied with; we're working to make it 100%.

Community Rooted

We're proud to be a local studio. We hire locally, source regionally where possible, and give back to the communities we serve.

Uncompromising Quality

We reject up to 30% of any flower shipment that doesn't meet our freshness standards. If it doesn't delight us, it won't reach you.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees, no surprise charges. What you see is what you pay — including VAT and delivery on orders over €60.

From farm to door

How We Work, Every Day

5:00 AM — Flower Market

Our florists arrive at the wholesale market before dawn, hand-selecting only the freshest and most vibrant stems of the day.

7:00 AM — Conditioning

Each flower is trimmed, stripped of excess foliage and placed in fresh water to hydrate and open naturally before arrangement.

9:00 AM — Arranging

Orders are hand-arranged by our florists, each bouquet uniquely composed with texture, colour and structure in mind.

11:00 AM — Packaging

Each arrangement is wrapped in our signature materials — tissue paper, branded box or ribbon — with a care card enclosed.

From 12:00 PM — Deliveries

Our drivers set out with orders, delivering to homes and offices across Riga and Rēzekne throughout the afternoon.
